Presented longitudinal analysis of chronic kidney disease using data from the ARDAC cohort linked with 20 administrative and national datasets. The analysis combined cohort, Medicare, pharmaceutical, hospital and mortality data to examine the cumulative incidence of CKD and patterns of health service use over more than two decades of follow-up. The findings highlighted gaps between screen-identified disease and recorded health service use, demonstrating how linked data can be used to generate insights into population health and inform earlier intervention.

The presentation was selected as a runner-up for the Kidney Health Australia Clinical Science Award, recognising the quality of the research presented in the Clinical Science Award session.
