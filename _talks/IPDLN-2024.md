Presented an analysis of kidney health using longitudinal data from the ARDAC cohort linked with national and state administrative datasets. The analysis demonstrated how linked data can be used to strengthen population health research and generate insights from multiple sources. Presented these findings to an international audience of data linkage researchers, communicating complex data and analytical findings in an accessible way.
